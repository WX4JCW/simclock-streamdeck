One per line: minutes before STD, then the text — 35 BOARDING or D-35 BOARDING. Leave it empty to use the list shown. Keep the text short; long lines shrink to fit.

Demo uses the PC clock and a pretend STD this many minutes from when it starts, so no SimBrief or MSFS is needed. On a dial, turn to move the demo clock a minute per click and walk through the whole departure. Hold the dial to restart it. The top line reads DEMO while it's on.

How it works

STD is scheduled off-block from your SimBrief OFP, counted against sim Zulu, the same as the Countdown to STD clock. The next reminder shows with the time until it. When it falls due the text turns the Due color and stays until you press to acknowledge it, or the next one falls due.

Live, turning the dial browses the list; it returns by itself after 10 seconds, or press to come back. With nothing due, a press reloads SimBrief. Holding the dial clears the acknowledgements. A new OFP clears them too.

Transparent lets whatever sits behind the clock show through — the Action Wheel's gradient, or a key image. The Background color is ignored while it's on.

Recolours the time on Countdown to STD as departure approaches — the header is left alone. Above every threshold it uses Time Color. Minutes before departure — plain (10) or clock notation (10:00, 1:30). Clear a row to switch it off.

Press the key or dial to reload SimBrief after regenerating a flight plan. Otherwise it refreshes on its own every 5 minutes. On an STD or countdown dial the press reloads instead of running the chronometer, and the dial does not turn.

Your simbrief.com login name — case-insensitive, without the @simbrief.com part. Entered once here and shared by every clock. Never your password.

SimBrief not showing a time?

Your SimBrief profile must have publicly visible enabled, or the API returns nothing. Log in to simbrief.com, open Account → Privacy, and confirm the toggle. You also need an OFP generated on that account.

STD is scheduled off-block from the OFP. Countdown to STD is that time minus sim Zulu, so it tracks time compression; once past it counts up as +MM:SS and the header reads LATE.

Sim time & fonts

Sim Zulu and the countdown read the simulator's clock. The plugin starts and stops the bridge for you — nothing to launch. With MSFS closed they show --:-- rather than substituting real time.

The Font list is every family installed on this PC, read from Windows. Weights appear as their own entries — Open Sans and Open Sans Light are separate choices. Leave it blank for Segoe UI.
